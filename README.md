# retired-but-not-useless
 🚀 RETIRED, BUT NOT USELESS

Their missions ended. Their contributions are not forgotten.

🌕 About the Project

Retired, But Not Useless is an interactive educational website developed for the NASA Space Apps Challenge. It explores the stories of NASA's lunar and Martian equipment that completed their missions and were left behind on the Moon and Mars.

Instead of viewing this equipment as forgotten machines, our project celebrates their contributions to space exploration and highlights the scientific discoveries and achievements they made possible.

🎯 Our Mission

Our goal is to make space exploration history interesting and accessible, especially for young learners and space enthusiasts. Through storytelling, interactive content, and engaging visuals, we aim to show that even after a mission ends, its contributions continue to matter.

✨ Key Features

- Memory Galaxy: Explore the legacy of space exploration.
- Life Journey: Discover the journey of NASA's equipment.
- Our Legends: Explore stories of four historic lunar and Martian equipment.
- Wisdom: Learn interesting facts and lessons from space exploration.
- Generations: Discover how past missions inspire future exploration.
- Interactive Experience: Explore educational content through an engaging, child-friendly interface.

🪐 Technologies Used

- HTML
- CSS
- JavaScript

🌍 Project Impact

This project encourages curiosity about space science and helps younger generations understand the importance of past space missions. It reminds us that equipment may stop working or complete its mission, but the knowledge gained from it continues to inspire humanity.

Their missions ended. Their stories didn't.

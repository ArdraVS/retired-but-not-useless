# retired-but-not-forgotten

🚀 RETIRED, BUT NOT USELESS

An educational website developed for the NASA Space Apps Challenge to explore the stories and contributions of NASA's lunar and Martian equipment left behind after completing their missions.

The current version offers an interactive, child-friendly experience with equipment profiles, interesting facts, and quizzes.

Future Scope: We aim to expand the platform with more equipment profiles, multimedia storytelling, interactive learning activities, and richer educational content.

Their missions ended. Their contributions are not forgotten.
